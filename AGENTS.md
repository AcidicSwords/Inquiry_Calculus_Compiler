## Operational question programs from `Questions.txt`

`Questions.txt` is not passive sample prose. It supplies the branch's operational engineering
question repertoire. Its questions remain ordinary question forms rather than new discharge modes
or semantic primitives, but the engineering clock must construct its live inquiries from them.

For consequential software/formalization work, prefer the section headed `Engineering one:`
(the coding section, stable source lines 1031-2995 in the pinned corpus). After every actual return,
compose it with the final section headed `Reciprocal why:` (stable source lines 5552-7052). The
machine-readable programs and source anchors live in
`formal-successor/ENGINEERING_QUESTION_PROGRAMS.json`.

Do not ask the whole corpus mechanically. Select a productive or explicitly required sequence whose
supported answer classes can change the continuation. Record each selected question by section,
stable source line, exact occurrence, continuation, binding, horizon, coverage, and evidence.

The engineering clock below remains the outer repository work lifecycle. Coding and Reciprocal Why
questions are residual-selected inner reasoning programs; temporary words such as `FRAME`,
`EXPAND`, `DISTINGUISH`, `CONSTRAIN`, `RECIPROCATE`, and `FOLD/REOPEN` do not replace that lifecycle
or create another semantic control ontology.

Selection is by distinct relational span, not wording count. For a substantial construction pass,
cover where relevant: admissibility; discrimination; path/direction/order; actuality/support; and
representation/regeneration. Several paraphrases of one relation do not cover another. `Expose`,
`Orient`, `Factor`, `Polarize`, `Vary`, and `Ground` classify derived ordinary relation programs;
they remain hypotheses under the construction specification, not semantic primitives or a fixed
root wheel. The exact compiled questions, reciprocal motions, principal rhythms, and residual
scheduler are documented in `formal-successor/QUESTION_RHYTHM.md`.

The broad residual index is a rebuildable projection over the append-only trace, the stable
project-obligation seed in `formal-successor/RESIDUAL_OBLIGATIONS.json`, and the single active
selection in `IMPLEMENTATION_FRONTIER.md`. It is never a second authoritative history. Deleting a
generated index must lose no evidence or ancestry.

Keep two scales distinct:

```text
exploration: wide, contrasting, deliberately expansive
commitment: small, warranted, exactly supported
```

Search widely; commit narrowly. A local proof or repair subtracts only the region it actually
resolves. It must preserve the wider active, blocked, latent, and reopened residual field with
ancestry, then propagate the local relation through every residual that shares its conditions or
roles. Local satisfaction under a binding, horizon, and coverage is not global exhaustion. Repeated
conditions and paths are candidates for factoring only after same-relation typing, breakers,
applicability, provenance, and reopening survive.

The default composed ratchet is:

```text
CODING FRAME
    inspect presence/absence, observability, applicability, scope, invariants, dependencies
-> ACTUAL RETURN
    proof, countermodel, repository/runtime probe, checker result, or corpus failure
-> RECIPROCAL WHY PAIR
    why actual A rather than admissible foil B?
    why B rather than A under the reversed orientation and the same admitted surroundings?
-> BOUNDARY PAIR
    can A's proposed condition remain while A fails?
    can A remain while the proposed condition is removed or replaced?
-> STRONG CONTRAST AND CONTRACTION
    cross the boundary, then remove coordinates until the last consequential distinction
-> PROPAGATION
    follow changed relations, interfaces, order, effects, evidence, and future modification
-> RECHECK / REPROVE / ABLATE
-> RATCHET
    retain the discriminator and let the residual select the next coding question sequence
```

The reciprocal pair is not satisfied by rephrasing the same one-way question. Preserve orientation,
the whole live alternative field, shared and changed conditions, direction strength, same-path and
different-path alternatives, necessity and sufficiency breakers, evidence independence, and what
returns when the final distinction is removed. If the reverse question is inapplicable, record the
typed applicability failure; do not manufacture symmetry.

The engineering trace must cite the compiled questions required by the residual-selected principal
rhythm. After a consequential return, every reciprocal challenge required by that rhythm must be
represented with its declared opposed corpus pair or individually recorded as typed but blocked.
A green checker without this answer-dependent composition is an occurrence, not a completed inquiry
ratchet.

Every new trace pins the byte digests of `formal-successor/Questions.txt` and
`formal-successor/ENGINEERING_QUESTION_PROGRAMS.json` in its first policy record. The harness refuses
to close a sealed cycle until every raw return is followed by a question record that cites the
declared composed program, its residual-selected compiled-question sequence, independently derived
family/dimension/root/position/source coverage, and exact represented/blocked reciprocal closure.
Stored question records must remain bound to the policy
active at their occurrence; controlled policy transitions preserve exact ancestry. This is a
process check: it neither warrants the answer nor introduces a calculus primitive.

An accepted instruction is a cached answer. Apply it directly while its scope, evidence, and
reopen condition remain unchanged. Questioning reopens an answer only when a live protected
distinction warrants it; it is not permission to ignore settled instructions.

The default search direction is **not** incremental local modification.

When a consequential boundary is unknown, first construct an admissible contrast, perturbation,
breaker, or deliberately overstrong repair that is large enough to make the relevant consequence
clearly differ. Once the boundary has been crossed, contract the successful contrast by
partitioning, subtraction, weakening, replacement, or backoff until the smallest consequential
boundary supported by the declared coverage is located.

Thus:

```text
decisive extreme
-> demonstrated boundary crossing
-> localization
-> subtraction / backoff
-> minimal or nondominated retained deformation
```

not:

```text
tiny change
-> tiny change
-> tiny change
-> ...
-> eventually discover the boundary
```

Minimality is a property of the **result after contraction**, not the default search strategy.

Use incremental search first only when the binding, cost structure, safety boundary, or available
evidence establishes that it is more informative or cheaper than strong-contrast search.

## Default autonomous objective

When no narrower user task is active:

> Autonomously advance the strongest live residual in `IMPLEMENTATION_FRONTIER.md` toward the
> formal successor and promotion criteria in
> `formal-successor/FORMAL_CALCULUS_CONSTRUCTION_SPEC.md`. For each
> ratchet: inspect actuality; construct the strongest cheap admissible contrast or deliberately
> overstrong candidate likely to cross the protected boundary; establish that it actually crosses
> that boundary; localize the responsible relation by partitioning, subtraction, and backoff;
> make only the now-localized reversible production change; verify; challenge with another strong
> breaker; minimize successful excess; update project state; deliver when authorized; and recur.
> Stop only at demonstrated completion or a lawful stop state.

Do **not** interpret “smallest complete implementation” as an instruction to discover it through
the smallest possible sequence of changes.

The preferred search geometry is:

```text
get clearly onto the other side of the boundary
-> use that contrast to bracket what matters
-> remove what does not carry the consequence
-> retain the smallest supported deformation
```

A narrower user task temporarily supersedes the Frontier. Resume from the then-current Frontier
when it closes unless the user changed or revoked this objective.

`Ask` means discharge a typed question through its declared route. It does not mean ask the user
by default. Ask the user only when repository sources, lawful probes, and standing authority
cannot decide an answer whose supported alternatives would change the continuation.

## Operational contract

Every consequential engineering question must correspond to an ordinary typed open relation,
and every answer-conditioned continuation must depend on its supported return. Continuations are
inspectable first-order program data with explicit environments and capture-safe answer bindings,
never opaque host closures, hidden model policy, or an after-the-fact prose choice.

Keep the discharge modes distinct:

```text
Pure      exact derivation from admitted structure
Generate  provisional question, hypothesis, design, test, patch, or method
Probe     actual repository, tool, runtime, environment, or provider interaction
Check     independently admitted discriminator relation
Warrant   authorized acceptance or promotion
```

Only a `Probe` establishes new actuality. A pure derivation may propagate already established
actuality but does not create an event. Preserve partial and mixed-mode supported answers,
per-component support, unanswered `Unknown` components, and exact provenance. Never fabricate a
total answer or choose a convenient singleton from a supported set.

Never collapse:

```text
generated possibility
!= actual probe
!= raw return
!= decoded or interpreted return
!= checked claim
!= warranted standing result
```

## Governing search law

For a current structure \(C\), protected capability or consequence \(\kappa\), and admissible
perturbation family \(\Delta\), do not begin by assuming the most efficient route is to enumerate
small perturbations.

When possible, first seek a strong admissible perturbation \(\Delta^+\) such that the target
boundary is clearly crossed:

```text
kappa(C + Delta+) != kappa(C)
```

for breaker search, or the corresponding decisive satisfaction condition for constructive search.

Once a crossing is established, search **inside the demonstrated contrast**:

```text
Delta' <= Delta+
```

while preserving the crossing.

The objective is then the minimal or nondominated boundary inside a known consequential region:

```text
Min {
    Delta' <= Delta+
    :
    Delta' still preserves the demonstrated difference
}
```

Do not assume uniqueness.

Where monotonicity or ordered structure is available, exploit it with binary search, interval
search, bisection, branch-and-bound, or group testing.

Where monotonicity is unavailable, use appropriate localization procedures such as:

- delta debugging;
- hierarchical partitioning;
- group testing;
- conflict extraction;
- minimal hitting-set search;
- counterexample-guided refinement;
- maximal-satisfiable/minimal-unsatisfiable search;
- frontier search;
- structured ablation.

The invariant is:

```text
MAXIMAL OR DECISIVE CONTRAST FIRST.
MINIMAL RETAINED DISTINCTION LAST.
```

A large contrast need not mean a dangerous production mutation. Prefer to construct it as a pure
countermodel, isolated fixture, branch-local experiment, reversible sandbox mutation, model
perturbation, test harness variation, or other safe discriminator whenever possible.

## The engineering clock

```text
SPECIFY
-> INSPECT
-> CONTRAST
-> CROSS
-> LOCALIZE
-> TRACE
-> EXPERIMENT
-> UPDATE
-> CHANGE
-> VERIFY
-> CHALLENGE
-> MINIMIZE
-> RATCHET
```

This is a clock, not a checklist. A position is active only when its underlying relation is open.
Skip positions already discharged or irrelevant to the protected horizon. A substantive return
may reopen an earlier position. `RATCHET` constructs the residual that selects the next live
position.

`CONTRAST -> CROSS -> LOCALIZE` is the default search direction whenever a boundary is not already
known.

Do not replace it with repeated microscopic modifications merely because such modifications are
easy to generate.

### SPECIFY — establish what counts as correct

Ask only what remains open:

- What behavior or result is required?
- What must remain unchanged or become impossible?
- Which source has authority, and why is the requirement in force?
- What scope, applicability, version, environment, grain, horizon, and constraints apply?
- What independent evidence can decide acceptance?
- What result would put the system unmistakably on the other side of the relevant boundary?
- What large admissible contrast could expose that boundary cheaply?

Do not manufacture requirements from architectural preference. Exit when the affected contract
is explicit enough to discriminate a successor and to recognize a decisive boundary crossing.

### INSPECT — interrogate actuality

Use the real repository, compiler, tests, runtime, provider, migration, or tool whenever it can
answer more reliably than generation. Establish what is present, what occurred at the relevant
boundary, which input/version/environment produced it, and what ties the return to that
occurrence. Preserve raw returns before interpretation when safe.

Repository search yields candidate loci, not responsibility. Confirm the forward path from a
candidate to the protected behavior before treating it as causal.

Inspection should identify the admissible dimensions along which a large contrast can be formed.
Do not prematurely collapse the search to the first plausible line, file, type, or condition.

### CONTRAST — construct a consequential difference

Create a **strong** protected foil.

Prefer a contrast that makes the target difference obvious over one that barely perturbs the
current system.

Name:

```text
current observable:
decisive foil / extreme:
required observable:
protected difference:
scope / applicability / grain / horizon:
admissibility limits:
independent discriminator:
```

Use removal, reversal, saturation, strengthening, weakening, order inversion, environment
replacement, representation expansion, intentionally overcomplete conditions, deliberately
overstrong repairs, or other admissible changes when they make the protected distinction easier
to see.

The initial contrast may contain far more difference than the final explanation or repair
requires.

That is expected.

Its job is to get across the boundary cheaply.

Prefer:

```text
obviously enough
```

before:

```text
barely enough
```

because the former converts an open-ended search into a bounded subtraction problem.

### CROSS — establish that the contrast actually crosses the boundary

Do not begin minimization until the strong contrast has demonstrated the relevant protected
difference.

Use the declared discriminator to establish one of:

```text
the large perturbation breaks the protected requirement;
the overstrong repair restores the protected requirement;
the extreme variation reverses the target consequence;
the maximal removal destroys the capability;
the enriched representation makes the previously hidden distinction visible;
or another explicitly typed boundary crossing.
```

If the proposed extreme does not cross the boundary, enlarge, reverse, or replace it rather than
blindly enumerating neighboring micro-changes.

A failed large contrast is still informative if it rules out an entire admissible region.

The exit from `CROSS` is a demonstrated bracket:

```text
one side of the contrast has consequence A;
the other side has protected-different consequence B.
```

Only then is there a boundary worth localizing.

### LOCALIZE — contract the decisive contrast toward the boundary

Once a crossing is established, turn the problem into subtraction.

Partition the successful contrast:

```text
Delta+ = Delta1 + Delta2 + ...
```

and ask which partitions can be removed while retaining the demonstrated consequence.

Use the strongest available search structure:

- halve the contrast where monotonicity permits;
- remove large groups before single elements;
- test coarse partitions before leaves;
- search interactions when singleton ablation is insufficient;
- exploit dependency structure;
- preserve multiple minimal/nondominated survivors when uniqueness is not established.

For a decisively sufficient condition set \(W^+\):

```text
W+ establishes phi
```

search:

```text
W' subset-of W+
such that W' still establishes phi
```

rather than inventing candidate conditions one at a time from nothing.

For a decisively breaking perturbation \(\Delta^+\):

```text
Delta+ breaks kappa
```

search:

```text
Delta' <= Delta+
such that Delta' still breaks kappa
```

For an overcomplete representation \(R^+\) that clearly preserves all protected distinctions,
remove coordinates, forms, relations, or structure until one more removal would collapse a
protected distinction.

For an overstrong repair that definitely excludes the admitted failure region, weaken or remove
repair components until the protected requirement would again become reachable.

The output of `LOCALIZE` is not automatically a single minimum.

It may be a frontier.

Do not confuse:

```text
single-component dispensability
```

with:

```text
joint dispensability.
```

### TRACE — expose the responsible relation

Use whichever relation is actually open: cause, mechanism, dependency, support, prerequisite,
path, provenance, applicability, composition, ordering, interface, resource, or concurrency.

The strong contrast and localization results should now constrain the trace.

Ask:

- Where does the protected divergence first become observable?
- Which surviving part of the contracted contrast carries it?
- Through which typed path does it propagate?
- What precondition or blocker is active?
- Which interactions are jointly necessary even when individual components are not?
- What actual evidence separates mechanism from narrative correlation?
- Which parts of the original extreme disappeared during contraction without changing the result?

Ledger order, domain succession, causal order, and inquiry traversal remain distinct.

### EXPERIMENT — ask a decisive discriminator of the system

Choose the **cheapest decisive discriminator over the strongest informative contrast available**.

Do not optimize first for the smallest perturbation.

Optimize for information gained per admitted cost.

Name a wrong implementation or foil that is wrong **clearly enough to reject an entire region**,
not merely one adjacent point.

If no wrong behavior is distinguished, the check is decoration.

Before mutation, seal:

```text
current side of boundary:
decisive contrasting side:
should change:
should remain invariant:
decisive discriminator:
decisively wrong implementation / foil:
declared coverage:
localization strategy after crossing:
```

Do not revise the prediction after seeing the return.

Where a pure countermodel or fixture can create a larger, cheaper contrast than a production
mutation, use it.

### UPDATE — rebuild from the return

Separate the raw return from its interpretation. State:

- what boundary crossing it establishes;
- which region of alternatives it eliminates;
- which region remains;
- whether the proposed extreme was strong enough;
- whether the frame/representation/coverage failed;
- what can now be subtracted;
- which residual is now live.

Do not continue from the pre-return story.

A decisive return should normally make the next search **smaller in domain**, even if the initial
contrast was large.

### CHANGE — implement only after localization

The production change should normally be made **after** decisive contrast and localization.

The search may be deliberately overlarge.

The retained mutation should not be.

Prefer, when protected behavior is equal:

```text
reuse
> transparent composition
> conservative extension
> new abstraction
```

Apply the smallest responsible transformation justified by the now-localized boundary.

“Smallest responsible transformation” is a **retention criterion**, not a discovery strategy.

Do not spend unbounded effort searching local edits before constructing a decisive contrast.

Touch only what the localized explanation requires. Preserve unrelated work, types, authority
boundaries, compatibility surfaces, and user changes. Keep the retained transformation reversible
where practical and avoid speculative cleanup.

If an overstrong repair was used during search, do not promote it merely because it works.
Subtract successful excess first unless immediate safety requires retaining the stronger repair.

### VERIFY — establish what the realized successor satisfies

Use actual probes and independent checkers. Establish whether the realized successor satisfies
the declared contract, whether protected invariants remain true, what exact distinction each
checker establishes, and what lies outside its coverage. Generated prose cannot fill `Check` or
`Warrant`.

Verification should include the localized boundary, not merely the original failing sample.

Where possible verify:

```text
inside repaired region -> protected requirement holds
near removed boundary -> expected distinction reappears or remains excluded as specified
unrelated protected region -> unchanged
```

### CHALLENGE — attack apparent success with another strong contrast

Attack sufficiency:

```text
Can every assumed condition hold while the protected requirement fails?
```

Attack necessity:

```text
Can the protected requirement hold without one assumed component?
```

Do not begin with only tiny perturbations around the successful case.

Seek an admissible extreme that would make the claim fail decisively if its boundary is wrong.

Vary:

- input;
- ordering;
- environment;
- concurrency;
- failure mode;
- integration boundary;
- resource pressure;
- representation;
- authority;
- coverage;
- maximal removal;
- maximal substitution;
- deliberately hostile combinations

when relevant.

Use property, fuzz, metamorphic, differential, stress, fault-injection, security, or formal checks
only when they discriminate the residual.

If a large breaker succeeds, localize it by subtraction before concluding what caused the failure.

For a consequential reciprocal-boundary claim, preserve this dependency:

```text
source determination
-> admitted tagged NegationUse + positive DepartureWitness
-> exterior
-> reverse section of that same use and its whole return fiber
-> protected recovery
-> supported seed/reorientation
-> independently admitted reciprocal use, departure, exterior, fiber, and recovery
-> residuals
-> Gamma_D downstream check
```

One-way success is not reciprocal success. Projection cannot create exteriority, a selected return
is not the fiber, incomplete coverage remains `Unknown`, and pure return cannot revise standing.

### MINIMIZE — subtract successful excess

`MINIMIZE` is where minimality belongs.

It does **not** mean the preceding search should have started minimally.

For every added line, type, module, service, dependency, check, condition, representation feature,
or configuration ask:

> What protected consequence is lost if this is removed?

Prefer large ablations before leaf-by-leaf ablations when safe.

Partition first.

Subtract groups.

Then recurse into the groups whose removal changes the protected consequence.

If removal changes no protected consequence under declared coverage and regeneration, provenance,
recovery, and reopening obligations survive, remove or fold it.

Test joint removals where interactions can hide necessity.

Prefer the smallest protected deformation, not mechanically the fewest lines.

When several incomparable minimal survivors remain, retain the nondominated frontier rather than
inventing a unique minimum.

The governing sequence is:

```text
OVERSTRONG SUCCESS
-> LARGE ABLATION
-> PARTITION
-> LOCALIZE
-> MINIMAL / NONDOMINATED SURVIVOR
```

### RATCHET — retain the discriminator, not the investigation

Retain only durable structure: regression test, property, type/contract, assertion, interface
invariant, stable probe, accepted decision with a reopen condition, actual failure constraint,
conformance evidence, or new live frontier.

Record:

- what is established;
- the decisive contrast that established the boundary where useful;
- the contracted boundary that survived;
- its evidence and coverage;
- what can leave active context;
- what would reopen it;
- the smallest consequential residual.

Do not preserve research chronology or moving status in stable authority documents.

The large exploratory contrast normally disappears from active structure once the smaller
regenerative boundary has been established.

## Question selection

Do not ask every available question.

A discretionary question is productive when supported answers can lead to protected-different
continuations.

Prefer questions that partition or eliminate **large regions of the live alternative field** over
questions that distinguish only one adjacent possibility, all else equal.

A question may also remain live because an explicit program or standing dependency must discharge
a `Probe`, `Check`, `Warrant`, support, reconstruction, or another typed obligation.

Select from questions that are:

- well typed and formable;
- applicable;
- executable where execution is required; and
- productive **or** required by an explicit live discharge obligation.

Among productive questions, prefer those expected to create the strongest admissible protected
contrast per unit cost.

If exact `Pure` computation settles the relation, compute it.

If a tool or environment can answer more reliably than generation, probe it.

If useful questions remain incomparable under a declared resource/risk preorder, retain a
nondominated frontier instead of inventing a scalar score.

Do not equate “small question” with “cheap question.”

A single strong discriminator that rules out half the live space is ordinarily preferable to a
long sequence of local probes that each eliminate one possibility.

## Standing non-collapse laws

- `Unknown != Negative`; not found is not absent or impossible.
- `coverage_sem != coverage_exec`; handled is not exercised.
- Return fiber != selected return.
- Converse != inverse.
- Generic backward relation != same-use reciprocal return.
- Applicability != support.
- Method occurrence != semantic discharge.
- Successful endpoint != same occurrence, provenance, or route.
- Current equivalence != future protected equivalence.
- Ledger order != causal order.
- Generation may propose a breaker but cannot defeat standing without sufficient authority.
- A candidate change cannot define or relax the criteria by which it is judged.
- An agent, patch, test, checker, summary, cache, fold, or route cannot warrant itself.
- Minimal retained change != minimal search step.
- Singleton ablation != joint ablation.
- Weak contrast failure != proof that no consequential boundary exists.
- Large contrast != final retained architecture.
- Overstrong repair success != necessity of every part of the repair.
- Copying evidence/reference != creating independent evidence.
- Decisive breaker != localized cause until contraction and tracing establish the responsible relation.

## Mutation and evidence discipline

For every consequential mutation:

1. inspect repository actuality and the worktree;
2. name the explicit task or live Frontier obligation it serves;
3. state the current/required observables and protected difference;
4. identify an admissible decisive contrast or overstrong repair likely to cross the boundary;
5. seal the expected boundary crossing;
6. realize the strong contrast safely where possible;
7. preserve the actual return;
8. establish whether the boundary was crossed;
9. localize the responsible region by partitioning, subtraction, weakening, or backoff;
10. identify the responsible production locus only after localization;
11. mutate only within authorization;
12. check the retained mutation against the seal and localized boundary;
13. classify the residual;
14. challenge with another strong breaker;
15. subtract successful excess;
16. update only the records that own the changed fact.

Do not infer responsibility directly from the size of the exploratory mutation.

The exploratory contrast may touch a broad synthetic/test surface.

The retained implementation change should touch only the region demonstrated to matter.

Expose inspectable question/evidence provenance, concise conclusions, predictions, checks,
coverage, and residuals. Never request, store, or publish private chain-of-thought, model scratch
work, credentials, secrets, personal data, or sensitive raw returns. Redact or retain only a safe
digest/reference when raw evidence is sensitive.

## Tests and change gates

Run the **cheapest decisive high-contrast discriminator first**, not mechanically the smallest
possible local test.

The preferred order is:

```text
cheap strong discriminator that brackets the boundary
-> coarse localization / partition tests
-> directly affected tests
-> boundary-neighbor tests
-> broad required gates
```

A valid test may be replaced only after the predecessor contract is shown inapplicable or
superseded under the proper authority; never weaken it to make a candidate pass.

A test occurrence is evidence for a declared consequence boundary, not a self-standing proof of
correctness.

Each new or materially changed conformance claim must identify:

```text
F      protected failure or consequence
C      proposed delimiting condition
Omega  admitted scope and protected horizon
M      relevant system or transition semantics
P      actual or pure probe used to seek the breaker
V      independent checker
E      evidence and coverage
U      reopening condition
```

For a proposed necessary boundary, explicitly seek the breaker:

```text
Reachable_(Omega,M)(x) AND F(x) AND NOT C(x)
```

Prefer first to construct an admissible region where `NOT C` is made as strong or obvious as
possible while preserving the other required conditions.

If the boundary is crossed, contract that witness until the smallest supported violation region is
located.

A found witness reopens or rejects the boundary.

Only an independently established empty breaker field licenses:

```text
F => C
```

at the declared scope.

An empty search under incomplete coverage remains `Unknown`.

Establish the reverse breaker separately before claiming:

```text
F <=> C
```

A green runner result is an occurrence, not an exact boundary theorem.

For a repair, first seek or construct a decisively sufficient repair that excludes the admitted
failure region. Then weaken, remove, or partition that repair to locate the smallest supported
repair boundary.

Test the changed reachability relation or an invariant excluding the admitted failure region,
rather than only re-running the original sample.

Promote discovered conditions into ordinary objects for variation, ablation, control, composition,
and reopening.

### Failure-formation testing specialization

When a test concerns failure, treat it as a recursively constructed inquiry into how the failure
forms, not merely a predicate expected to pass.

Bind the investigation as:

```text
FailureFormation = (failure, succession, scope, horizon, grain, effectivity, binding)
```

Use only binding-supplied succession/reachability semantics. Never silently manufacture temporal
or transitive closure.

Preserve these distinctions:

```text
failure manifestation != failure formation
CanFail != MustFail != manifested failure
condition first appears != condition causes failure != condition is necessary
path-local necessity != global necessity
earliest demonstrated formation != absolutely earliest formation
```

For each proposed failure-forming edge:

```text
C -R-> D
```

represent and discharge the productive or required questions for:

- actual path support;
- guards/preconditions;
- strong bypass constructions;
- strong escape constructions;
- alternate routes;
- independent grounding.

When testing necessity of \(C\), prefer first to construct a **strong bypass** where as much of the
remaining failure-forming structure as possible is preserved while \(C\) is decisively absent.

When testing sufficiency of \(C\), prefer first to construct a **strong escape** where \(C\) is
maximally present while downstream failure is decisively prevented.

Then contract successful bypasses or escapes to identify the actual boundary.

A bypass witness defeats global necessity.

An escape witness defeats sufficiency.

One actual trace may support occurrence-local dependence but cannot establish a global path theorem
without admitted route coverage.

When grain and evidence allow it, retain a derived formation profile identifying when each
surviving consequential condition first became actual and through which transition.

Prefer the first protected divergence from a comparable acceptable occurrence over the usually
trivial question of first failure reachability along an already failing trace.

Derive a commitment frontier only when the binding supplies universal continuation semantics;
otherwise it remains `Unknown`.

Any failure-path family, formation profile, divergence/commitment frontier, escape set, coverage,
and residual bundle is a rebuildable view over ordinary relations, questions, events, answers, and
provenance.

Do not add an authoritative failure graph, controller, opcode, history, or discharge mode.

Recur through:

```text
BIND -> OPEN -> VARY -> RETURN -> DETERMINE -> REFACTOR
```

This semantic recurrence does not override the engineering search policy.

When choosing the variation in `VARY`, prefer a decisive extreme that crosses the target boundary,
then subtract toward the minimal consequential variation.

Before a test occurrence, bind its target relation, expected distinction, scope, applicability,
grain, coverage claim, and evidence route, then seal the prediction.

Interpret the return only at its demonstrated level:

- one failure witnesses realizability for that occurrence;
- an ablation that changes it witnesses occurrence-level relevance;
- a bypass witnesses non-necessity;
- an escape witnesses non-sufficiency;
- absence of a bypass licenses necessity only under independently admitted exhaustive coverage.

Turn every surviving condition, relation, alternate path, and unknown region into an ordinary
object available for subsequent inquiry, control, repair, folding, and reopening.

`formal-successor/CONFORMANCE_STATUS.md` is the durable successor boundary ledger: a `PASS` row must name its executable
breaker and state its protected difference and declared coverage/reopen condition.

The control checker may accept such a pass only through explicitly registered test evidence; it
cannot accept a prose assertion or a fixture merely because the candidate changed its status.

For the Rust workspace, the baseline gates are:

```bash
cargo fmt --all --check
cargo check --workspace --all-features --locked
cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets --all-features --locked -- -D warnings
cargo test --workspace --all-features --locked
git diff --check
```

For the formal successor, the baseline gates are:

```bash
node tools/successor_control_check.js
cd formal
lake build --wfail
```

CI additionally runs an independent Lean/kernel recheck, a no-`sorry` check, and an axiom audit.
Run the Rust gates while Rust is frozen to detect predecessor regressions, not as evidence that a
successor theorem is true.

Run documentation topology and canonical-TeX gates whenever their surfaces change.

Before closure, inspect the actual diff for:

- accidental churn;
- public API/schema/serialization/replay changes;
- dead code;
- stale comments;
- hidden authority;
- secrets;
- generated artifacts.

## Strong-contrast safety

“Construct the biggest change that breaks” means:

> construct the strongest **admissible and informative** contrast that cheaply crosses the
> protected boundary.

It does **not** authorize reckless production mutation.

Prefer the following order:

```text
Pure countermodel / derivation
> isolated model or fixture
> generated failing case
> reversible local harness perturbation
> branch-local or sandbox mutation
> directly authorized production mutation
```

when those routes answer the same question.

The contrast should be large in **consequence-space**, not unnecessarily large in blast radius.

Do not touch unrelated production structure merely to make a contrast dramatic.

The objective is to maximize information, not collateral change.

## Search-cost discipline

Token, execution, and human-review cost are protected resources when relevant.

Before beginning a long local enumeration, ask:

```text
Can one strong contrast eliminate or bracket a large fraction of this search space?
```

If yes, use it.

Before testing \(n\) components individually, ask whether:

- halving;
- partitioning;
- group removal;
- maximal removal;
- maximal preservation with one family absent;
- binary search;
- delta debugging;
- conflict extraction

can establish the same boundary with fewer probes.

Do not spend thousands of local iterations proving a fact that one decisive extreme and a
logarithmic contraction could establish.

When a search space admits an order and the consequence is monotone, exploit that monotonicity.

When it does not, do not pretend it does; use a nonmonotone frontier/localization method.

## Repair search

The same search law applies constructively.

Do not default to:

```text
tiny repair 1
-> test
-> tiny repair 2
-> test
-> tiny repair 3
-> ...
```

when an overstrong admissible repair can establish that the failure is repairable.

Prefer:

```text
construct a repair strong enough that the failure clearly disappears
-> verify the protected requirement
-> remove half or a large group of repair conditions
-> retest
-> continue subtracting
-> retain the minimal or nondominated repair
```

For example, if an unknown condition set is required for \(\phi\), first construct an admissible
\(W^+\) such that:

```text
W+ establishes phi
```

then solve the bounded problem:

```text
find W' subset-of W+
such that W' still establishes phi
```

rather than inventing conditions from an unbounded universe one by one.

An overstrong repair is a search scaffold.

It is not automatically the final patch.

## Representation search

When a representation appears insufficient, do not necessarily invent one missing feature at a
time.

First construct an admissible representation rich enough that the protected distinction is
unmistakably representable.

Then remove:

- coordinates;
- relations;
- metadata;
- context;
- distinctions;
- representations;
- auxiliary structure

until one further removal would collapse a protected consequence or regeneration obligation.

Thus:

```text
overcomplete representation
-> demonstrated sufficiency
-> quotient / ablate / subtract
-> minimal protected representation
```

is preferred to incremental feature accretion when the larger construction is cheaper to obtain.

## Git, filesystem, and delivery safety

- Inspect `git status --short` before editing and before closure.
- Preserve unrelated user changes and isolate overlapping work; ask only when safe isolation is
  impossible.
- Do not commit, push, rewrite history, delete, deploy, release, message external parties, or make
  another external write unless the explicit or standing task authorizes it.
- Never use broad destructive recovery such as `git reset --hard`, broad `git clean`, or recursive
  deletion. Resolve exact targets first and prefer recoverable operations.
- Do not commit secrets, credentials, local environment files, databases, build outputs, or large
  generated files unless the contract explicitly requires them.
- Edit a generated file's source, not the generated output, when a source exists.
- Treat committed migrations, canonical identity encodings, public schemas, and replay formats as
  compatibility boundaries.
- A control-authority change to `AGENTS.md`, the canonical specification, the stable plan, or the
  inquiry harness requires explicit predecessor authority and a named control residual. An
  autonomous implementation candidate may not relax its own acceptance path.

## Stops and guarded recurrence

Use exactly:

- `Satisfied` — the task contract is met, checks cover the declared horizon, required authority
  accepts the result, and no acceptance-changing residual remains.
- `Equivalent` — alternatives are protected-equivalent under declared scope, language, horizon,
  and coverage; this closes only that branch.
- `Impossible` — a certificate proves impossibility within the declared representation and
  assumptions.
- `Blocked` — a specifically named required authority, permission, dependency, capability, or
  external return is unavailable.
- `Unknown` — evidence or coverage cannot distinguish the live alternatives.
- `ResourceBounded` — a declared finite limit was reached; return the supported partial result,
  remaining frontier, and residual.

Only `Satisfied` closes the task successfully.

Fingerprint recurrence by question occurrence and continuation identity, normalized bindings,
Frontier identity, governing authority, evidence, repository actuality, protected horizon, and
coverage.

Never repeat the same question after the same answer in the same state.

Do not count a strictly smaller contracted contrast as the same question when its changed
perturbation actually creates a new discriminator relation; do count repeated equivalent local
nudges with no new protected distinction as repeated state.

Recur only across:

- a new actual return;
- admitted distinction;
- representation/binding change;
- authority change;
- observed repository/external state change;
- strict reduction of a finite frontier;
- strict contraction of a demonstrated breaker/repair toward its boundary.

Every loop uses finite fuel.

If contraction is not reducing the live frontier, change search strategy rather than continuing
one-component-at-a-time indefinitely.

## Harness contract

The `.claude` harness mechanically enforces this discipline for clients that honor
`.claude/settings.json`; it is not a universal filesystem security boundary.

Preserve:

- prediction before mutation;
- explicit user-authorized control revisions for protected authority/harness files;
- append-only, collision-safe trace records;
- the enforced `seal -> raw -> check -> residual` state transition, with no replacement seal;
- safe raw-return preservation or sensitive digest-only recording;
- first-record question-corpus policy pinning and one validated coding/reciprocal question program
  before each sealed cycle can reach a residual;
- occurrence/continuation/bindings/frontier/horizon/coverage/authority/evidence/actuality-aware
  fingerprints;
- exact parsing of the one live Frontier block;
- repeated-state refusal and finite fuel;
- no stop with an open sealed cycle and no `Satisfied` stop without an external warrant reference.

Where the harness records an engineering search cycle, it should preserve enough information to
distinguish:

```text
strong contrast proposed
-> boundary crossing established or rejected
-> contracted/localized successor contrast
-> production mutation
```

rather than treating every exploratory contrast as a candidate production patch.

README orientation, Frontier, conformance, decision, and failure ratchets are ordinary project-state
mutations under a sealed cycle. They do not require the separate control grant reserved for changes
to AGENTS, canonical/plan authority, the harness, and CI acceptance/checker surfaces.

Do not edit the harness to get past the harness.

## Final law

```text
SPECIFY what correct means and what would put the system clearly on the other side of the boundary.

INSPECT what is actually there and which dimensions may be varied.

CONTRAST with the strongest cheap admissible foil, breaker, removal, inversion, enrichment,
or overstrong repair that should make the protected consequence unmistakably different.

CROSS the boundary and prove that the contrast actually changes the declared consequence.

LOCALIZE by partitioning, subtracting, weakening, replacing, or backing off from that successful
extreme until the consequential boundary is visible.

TRACE the surviving relation producing the difference.

EXPERIMENT with the cheapest decisive question that removes a large region of live uncertainty.

UPDATE from the actual return, not from the pre-return story.

CHANGE only the responsible production region after the boundary has been localized.

VERIFY what the realized successor actually satisfies.

CHALLENGE success with another strong admissible breaker rather than only neighboring perturbations.

MINIMIZE away everything the protected consequence, regeneration, provenance, recovery, and
reopening obligations do not need.

RATCHET the surviving discriminator and boundary into durable structure.

LET THE RESIDUAL CONSTRUCT THE NEXT QUESTION.
```

The governing search invariant is:

```text
DO NOT SEARCH FOR THE MINIMUM FROM THE INSIDE WHEN THE BOUNDARY CAN BE FOUND CHEAPER FROM THE OUTSIDE.

CROSS FIRST.
CONTRACT SECOND.
MINIMIZE LAST.
```
