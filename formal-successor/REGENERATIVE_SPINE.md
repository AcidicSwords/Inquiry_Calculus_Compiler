# Regenerative spine

`REGENERATIVE_SPINE.json` is the compact, rebuildable dependency projection requested for the
formal successor. It does not add a controller, history, semantic authority, or second live
frontier. `IMPLEMENTATION_FRONTIER.md` remains the authoritative cursor, the integrated theorem
registry remains the planned proof/break schedule, and Lean returns remain the mathematical
evidence.

The projection maps each reciprocal-regenerative candidate to the exact current predecessor
surface it would change, its present meaning, the stronger proposed relation, the question that
makes that relation an answer, required types and hypotheses, current derivation attempt, missing
structure, strongest known breaker, disposition, propagation surface, v2.0 preservation condition,
and primitive-elimination consequence.

The present comparison does **not** warrant the proposed successor. It establishes three useful
architectural constraints now:

- `Orient` is one of six roots with two orientations, not two primitives; reification adds no
  seventh root.
- semantic transport, reciprocal mates, and paired-path consequences are candidates derivable
  from relational composition, while actual answer-dependent succession stays distinct.
- determination, quotient maximality, and the converse reopening witness require explicit
  hypotheses that must not be hidden by cleaner notation.

`node tools/regenerative_spine_check.js` validates every artifact edge, theorem-obligation edge,
allowed disposition, non-collapse law, attachment digest, and exact synchronization with the one
live frontier.
