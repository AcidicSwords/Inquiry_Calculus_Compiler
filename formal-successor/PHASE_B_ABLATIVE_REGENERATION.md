A shaped ablation retains the entire typed presentation and names the target
form. Exactly its protected occurrences become open. The filler field is the
predicate of forms satisfying the typed port and every retained governing
relation at every opened occurrence. `regenerationField` is definitionally that
whole solution predicate, not a selected return.

Protected quotient singletonhood is computed from a supplied equivalence Setoid:
the surviving quotient-class predicate must equal the singleton class of the
target. In the finite contrast both Boolean fillers solve the same shaped hole.
Under the all-equivalent protection they induce one target quotient class; under
equality protection they induce two classes. Raw cardinality therefore neither
establishes nor refutes the direct protected witness.

The formal relation performs no search or execution. It does not delete an
occurrence, erase governing relations, choose a filler, reuse the older opaque
singleton flag as evidence, emit an event, change Rust, or promote successor
semantics.
