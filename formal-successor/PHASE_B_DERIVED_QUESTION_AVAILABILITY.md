4908–4941: one `FormalDefinition`, 15 `Ambiguous` `LegacyObligation` records, and two `Unproved`
`LegacyObligation` records. The finite typed model retains formability, applicability, execution,
answerability, productivity, resolvedness, readiness, and required discharge as distinct relations.

The decisive contrasts are deliberately varied: a formable question is out of scope; a formable
question has no executable route; an executable exhaustive probe has no supported completion; an
otherwise equivalent-answer occurrence has insufficient coverage; and a formable applicable
question lacks its use contract. A required check is nonproductive, while a productive occurrence
has no standing dependency requiring its discharge.

Required discharge reuses the declared Probe, Check, Warrant, exact-support, and reconstruction
route carrier. It neither creates a question species nor asserts executability, successful
discharge, authorization, a controller, or a scheduler. The finite model is source-bound evidence,
not a successor definition or a Gate-B pass. `node tools/derived_question_availability_check.js
--compile` verifies the source binding, rejects eight targeted ablations, and audits nine
axiom-free proofs.
