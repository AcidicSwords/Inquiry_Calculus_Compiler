Ablation removes a supplied set of active distinctions and definitionally retains
the ancestry. A supplied protected contract separately requires live-completion
separation, inquiry regeneration, and licensed continuation.

Subtraction is admitted only while that full contract remains satisfied. A
locally irreducible presentation requires, for every further active single
removal, the propositional existence of typed positive loss evidence. The five
source dimensions—regeneration, discrimination, continuation, warrant
provenance, and reopening—are retained as loss tags. The supplied loss relation

distinction. Removing the redundant coordinate preserves the full contract and
ancestry, so the over-distinguished representation is not yet “only enough.” The
contracted two-coordinate representation is locally irreducible: removing one
coordinate has a discrimination witness; removing the other has a regeneration
witness.

This is a conditional finite theorem, not proof of the original law at all
bindings. It installs no universal minimizer, destructive history operation,
automatic engineering fold, executor, Rust correspondence, or successor
primitive.
